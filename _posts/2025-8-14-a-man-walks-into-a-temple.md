Saturday, February 25, 2022. [Port Blair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Blair). 

It's dusk, and I'm heading back to my BnB from a successful street photography session. 

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-01.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-02.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-03.jpg)

I'm only a few hundred meters from my room when I see a temple. There's commotion inside. I linger for a bit, hesitant, then decide to walk in. 

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-04.jpg)*A woman offers turmeric and vermilion to devotees*

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-05.jpg)*A priest*

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-06.jpg)*Another priest*

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-07.jpg)*A woman priest*

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-08.jpg)*The head priest*

It's cramped, but not suffocating. Something has overcome the people. Is it God? Is it the environment? Is it energy? What do I call it?

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-09.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-10.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-11.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-12.jpg)

I consider myself a man of science, but I can't help but be fascinated. It doesn't seem fake... No, definitely not. Then what is it? I try to find the right word to describe it. It's tempting (or rather instinctive) to reach out for "possessed." But "possessed" sounds wrong, like they're being forced. They're *not* being forced. They're enchanted. Of their own will. They're surrendering. Yes, it's "surrender."

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-13.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-14.jpg)*Mesmerized.*

Bodies touch but don't withdraw. Men, women, kids. Everyone touches everyone. People touch me. I touch people. A woman rests her hand on my shoulder, craning her neck to peek over the crowd.  I'm surprised, but only for a moment. It all oddly feels natural. As if this is how it was supposed to be.

I've made friends with some of the kids. 

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-15.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-16.jpg)*This little guy's swag was on a whole different level 🤙*

Keerthi is a mischievous little girl. She loves her big brother and wants a picture with him. She runs off into the crowd to find him. They pose, she gets her picture. 

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-17.jpg)*Keerthi with her brother*

Then she takes my camera and becomes a professional photographer.

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-18.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-19.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-20.jpg)

There's another kid I become good friends with. I can't remember his name. Let's call him Murugan. He's a bit older, maybe 10-12. He thinks he's responsible for the younger kids. I sense a protector-and-overseer energy from him.

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-21.jpg)*That's Murugan in the center*

I stay there well into the night. Shooting, talking, observing, having fun. 

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-22.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-23.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-24.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-25.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-26.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-27.jpg)

They're going to make a sacrifice at 4am and there will be a feast. The head priest has invited me to stay. But my camera's battery is exhausted. So is mine. I go back to my room to recharge.

<head priest climbs onto a bridge of swords, some kind of ritual>

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-28.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-309.jpg)

![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/2025-8-14-a-man-walks-into-a-temple/andaman-post-29.jpg)*This is a ritual where the head priests climbs onto a bridge of swords and makes a proclamation*

<center>*</center>

I wake up in the morning and head out.

I look for my young friends from last night. Murugan's there. He tells me Keerthi had been looking for me. The festivities of the night are over and I everyone's lined up for the feast. 

We talk for a while and I bid adieu. Suddenly Murugan gives me a hug. I'm taken by surprise, but just for a second. The feeling of surprise quickly morphs into others - I feel accepted. I feel loved. I feel like I belong. I feel... happy.
