_This is a collection of my favorite lines from every episode of [1883](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt13991232/), Taylor Sheridan's masterpiece from the [Yellowstone](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt4236770/) universe. This show is basically [Lonesome Dove](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/256008.Lonesome_Dove) for women that men can watch too, but I'd be an asshole if I reduced it to just that. It's so much more. It touches you, speaks to you, fondles, consoles, caresses, pulls, tugs, hits you, stabs you, and kills you from every angle and direction to make you feel the entirety of the "human experience." The writing's good, in the sense that it has a classical literary beauty and elegance. That combined with the live-off-the-land-in-connection-with-nature life of the characters, the focus on human emotion, and special emphasis on the themes of freedom, wonder, courage, and agency, this is a treat for the old-school bibliophile types. Don't scroll to the end if you don't want major spoilers._
